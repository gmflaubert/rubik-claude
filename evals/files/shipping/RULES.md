# Shipping rules (from the ops team, pasted as-is)

1. Domestic orders under 1 kg cost a flat 5.00.
2. Domestic orders from 1 kg up to 5 kg cost 9.00.
3. Domestic orders over 5 kg cost 9.00 plus 2.00 for each extra full kg above 5.
4. International orders cost double the domestic price for the same weight.
5. Orders of exactly 5 kg ship at the "over 5 kg" rate.
6. Any order with a subtotal of 50.00 or more ships free.
7. International orders never ship free.
8. Fragile items add 4.00 per order, regardless of weight, and are never free-shipping eligible.
